Mandelbrot zoom

Click to zoom in three times, centred where you click. Each view is drawn coarse first and refined pass by pass: every frame computes for a few milliseconds, writes the RGBA buffer to the canvas with one Dom.put_pixels, and reads the clicks that came in, so a new click interrupts a render at once.

click zoom in · right-click or - zoom out · + zoom in at the centre · R reset 







Source · mandelbrot-zoom/main.kai





Compiled from kaikai to WebAssembly with kai build --target=wasm32-browser. Needs JSPI: Chrome/Edge 137, Firefox 153, Safari 27.
